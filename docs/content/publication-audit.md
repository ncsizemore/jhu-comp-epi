# Publication audit and proposed archive

## Result

The corrected proposal contains 19 distinct papers and preprints: all 14 previously visible studies, corrected where necessary, plus five additions. Eleven TB or PEARL papers added in the first audit pass have been removed following the user’s group-scope clarification. The design and search interaction are retained. All coauthors are now searchable, with full citation author lists available under each entry. The preceding [14-record page](publications-v9.html) and its [source snapshot](publications-data-v9.json) are preserved.

This is a bounded editorial audit, not a systematic or exhaustive bibliography. It covers the existing source list, group-led modeling, the faculty profile, and a targeted recent-author search through 19 October 2026. It does not import every faculty coauthorship. Search indexing can lag publication. Journal citation years are used consistently; online-first years can differ. The full source metadata and search terms are in [publication-evidence.json](publication-evidence.json).

## Confirmed group boundary

TB belongs to a separate group. PEARL also remains a separate group; do not include its papers unless its PI agrees. Research overlap, shared authors, and a faculty profile are insufficient grounds for attributing work to CIPHER. This supersedes the initial audit’s recommendation to restore PEARL records or add TB studies. Keep the excluded citation evidence for provenance, not as a source of automatically eligible additions.

Aging research is not synonymous with PEARL: the JHEEM US aging analysis and existing Kenya HIV intervention/aging study remain. The Research overview no longer presents TB as part of this group’s portfolio; individual researcher expertise is a separate matter.

## Pruning and remaining scope decisions

Recommend pruning duplicate versions, not age. Keep the foundational 2016–2021 modeling papers: they establish continuity and are useful to researchers. Chronological ordering already gives current work priority. The 2018 Spain application and 2020 multi-country PrEP study are the most reasonable candidates to remove from a deliberately narrow selected-work list; neither is intrinsically irrelevant to a group research archive. They remain in this proposal pending that scope decision.

The hidden Edwards 2022 mortality analysis (DOI 10.1093/cid/ciab1030) is a cohort study rather than a core model output. Leave it off this proposed modeling-focused archive, but retain it in the source inventory. The 2026 AJE weight-gain intervention study (DOI 10.1093/aje/kwag255), 2026 Lancet HIV ageing Commission (DOI 10.1016/S2352-3018(26)00058-5), HIV integration commentary, and other faculty epidemiology or methods collaborations need the same editorial scope decision. The new AJE paper is distinct from the excluded PEARL BMI simulation paper, not a duplicate. Any proposed addition must first satisfy the group-ownership boundary. Do not treat exclusion here as a judgment of scientific quality.

No unverified SHIELD publication is invented to fill a visual gap. Conference abstracts are outside this pass. Several search hits for Fojo relate to an oncology researcher; shared surname or initials are not enough to attribute work to this group.
